Proyectos

Lumina DeFi App - Vara Main Hackathon

During the hackathon, we initiated the development of Lumina, a DeFi application focused on peer-to-peer microloans. Lumina introduces a pitch-in model that enables collective lending to small businesses, while also integrating additional financial features to enhance accessibility and transparency.

Throughout this experience, our team explored decentralized programming, blockchain technology, and deepened our understanding of Web 3.0 concepts, working in collaboration with Vara Networks to bring this vision to life.
